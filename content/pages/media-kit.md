## What is Nectar Network?

Nectar Network is exit infrastructure for lending markets: it lines up funded buyers for collateral so liquidations can clear when DEX liquidity can't absorb them. It is in development, with a small pilot cohort of curators, lending protocols and buyers.

## How should I refer to it?

Use **Nectar Network** on first mention, and **Nectar** after that. The protocols underneath are **Pappus** (settlement and quote escrow) and **Macaulay** (pricing for collateral that redeems later).
