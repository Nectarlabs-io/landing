Everything you need to write about Nectar Network: how to refer to it, a one-line description, the mark and lockup, app icons, colours and usage rules. Every file is linked below in SVG, PNG and JPG. For anything else, email kunal@nectarnetwork.fun.

## What is Nectar Network?

Nectar Network is credit and liquidity for tokenized assets: lending markets whose borrowing limits are backed by committed buyers, so loans can go further and positions still close when DEX liquidity can't absorb them. It is in development, with a small pilot cohort of buyers, lenders and apps.

## How should I refer to it?

Use **Nectar Network** on first mention, and **Nectar** after that. The protocols underneath are **Pappus** (settlement and quote escrow) and **Macaulay** (pricing for collateral that redeems later).

## What does the mark look like?

The mark is a honeycomb of seven cells: six hairline cells frame one lit accent cell, which holds the nectar droplet. One brand system runs through everything: electric mint on near-black, with Syne and DM Mono.

- Use the primary mark at 32px and above.
- Below 32px, switch to the compact mark, which has a solid centre cell, so it stays legible.
- Never recolour the droplet. It is always the accent mint.

## What is the lockup?

The lockup is the mark with the wordmark: **NECTAR** above a smaller **NETWORK**. The primary lockup uses the mint mark; the monochrome lockup is a single-colour reversal for dense layouts and one-ink printing.

The wordmark is set in Syne 700, tracked 0.2em, with the NETWORK sub-label in DM Mono. Keep the gap between the mark and the wordmark, and never substitute the typeface.

## How much clear space does the mark need?

Keep clear space of one cell on every side of the mark or lockup. Nothing should cross that boundary.

| Use | Minimum size |
| --- | --- |
| Primary mark | 32px; never smaller |
| Compact mark | 16px to 24px, for favicons and small UI |

The mark is designed for dark backgrounds. On light or photographic backgrounds, use the monochrome mark, as a white knockout or in a single ink. The mint accent never sits on white.

## What shouldn't I do with the mark?

- Don't distort or stretch it.
- Don't rotate it.
- Don't recolour it, or the droplet.
- Don't place the mint mark on white.
- Don't put gradients behind it.
