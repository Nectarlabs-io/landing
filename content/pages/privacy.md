The only personal data this site collects is what you send through the [pilot form](/contact). We use it to reply to you about the pilot, nothing else, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing kunal@nectarnetwork.fun.

## What does the pilot form store?

When you send the form, we store:

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your role, name, work email and organisation | So we know who you are and can reply |
| Chains, markets or collateral, size involved and message, if you give them | So we can prepare for a call about your markets |
| Telegram or X handle, if you give one | So we can reach you another way if you prefer |
| Your browser's user agent | To spot automated submissions |
| A one-way hash of your IP address | To limit how many requests one network can send |
| The date and time you sent it | To keep requests in order |

We never store your IP address itself. We store a salted SHA-256 hash of it, which lets us count requests from the same network without being able to recover the address.

## What happens after you send it?

The request is saved to our database, and a notification with its contents is emailed to kunal@nectarnetwork.fun so we can reply. Replying to that email goes straight to you. We don't add you to a mailing list, and there is no newsletter.

## Who else handles your data?

Two services handle it on our behalf: Railway, which hosts this site and its database, and Resend, which delivers the notification email. We don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

## How long do we keep it?

We keep a pilot request for 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete it. If you'd like it deleted sooner, ask us and we will.

## How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Email kunal@nectarnetwork.fun from the address you used on the form and ask us to delete your request. We'll delete it, before the 12 months are up, and reply to confirm.

## Does this site use cookies or analytics?

No. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Your choice of dark or light theme is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only, and is never sent to us.

## Who is responsible for this data?

Nectar Network. Questions about this page go to kunal@nectarnetwork.fun.
