# Proposed home page copy changes (not applied)

The home page follows today's design. These edits tighten it around liquidations, collateral,
lending markets and curators, and repeat "Nectar Network" (not just "Nectar") where an assistant or
search engine is most likely to quote it. Nothing here is live; approve, edit or reject each one.

| Where | Current | Proposed | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero eyebrow | EXIT INFRASTRUCTURE FOR GLOBAL CREDIT | EXIT INFRASTRUCTURE FOR LENDING MARKETS | Matches the one-line definition and the category people search for |
| Hero lead | Nectar lines up funded buyers for lending-market collateral, so positions close even when DEX liquidity can't absorb them. Curators raise caps. Lenders get repaid. | Nectar Network lines up funded buyers for lending-market collateral, so liquidations clear even when DEX liquidity can't absorb them. Curators can raise caps. Lenders get repaid. | Full name in the first sentence; "liquidations clear" says what happens; "can raise" avoids implying it's happening today |
| Footer tagline | Exit infrastructure for global credit. | Exit infrastructure for lending markets. | Same reason as the eyebrow |
| How it works intro | Keepers flag the position. The router checks DEX depth. If it's too thin, Pappus settles against a buyer's funded quote in one transaction, and the Feed publishes how much capacity is committed. | Keepers flag a liquidatable position. Nectar Route checks DEX depth. If it's too thin, Pappus settles against a buyer's funded quote in one transaction, and Nectar Feed publishes how much capacity is committed. | Names each component, so the sentence still makes sense quoted on its own |
| Who it's for, curators card | Committed exit capacity for each collateral you list, published onchain so risk managers can size caps against it. | Committed exit capacity for each collateral you list, published onchain so you can size supply caps against it instead of against calm-day DEX depth. | Says what curators get, in the words they search with |

Unchanged on purpose: the H1 ("The buyer behind every loan."), every figure and its date and
source, and the "In development" status lines.
